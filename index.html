About the Workshop

JOBS workshop is aimed at helping senior graduate students and postdocs prepare for their upcoming job searches. JOBS is targeted towards those seeking academic or industry careers and will feature a series of talks, panels, and networking opportunities for participants. The main goals of this workshop are: (1) provide helpful advice and mentorship to graduate students and postdocs who will soon be on the job market, and (2) provide visibility to JOBS candidates & connect them with potential employers in academia and industry.
