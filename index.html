About the Workshop

The workshop has two aims. The first is advice: panels and a talk on how the academic and industrial job markets work, from people who have recently been through them and from people who make hiring decisions. The second is visibility: all registered attendees will be listed as job candidates on this site for potential employers attending {{ w.conference_name }}.

Panels and Talks

A "Tips from the Trenches" panel on wrapping up your PhD and applying for jobs, an "Architecture Globally" panel featuring professors and industry experts from around the world, and a talk offering a department chair's perspective on hiring.

Mini-Workshop

An interactive mini-workshop on crafting your CV, elevator pitch, and research/teaching statements.

Coffee Break

Time set aside for candidates to meet the panelists and potential employers attending the conference.
