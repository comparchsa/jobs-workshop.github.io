## Attend

The workshop is open to all {{ w.conference_name }} registrants and is included
in conference registration. There is no separate workshop fee and no RSVP —
turn up on the day.

It is aimed at senior PhD students and postdocs preparing for the job market,
and anyone else at the conference is welcome.

## Apply as a job candidate

Applications go through the workshop's submission site. The organizers review
them and select the candidates who are listed on the
[job candidates page]({{ '/candidates/' | relative_url }}) and introduced during
the workshop.

{% include cta.html %}

Deadlines are listed under important dates on the
[home page]({{ '/' | relative_url }}).

## Speak

Panelists are drawn from three groups:

- Senior researchers in industry research, product engineering, and academia.
- Recent hires who went through the market in the last year or two.
- People who have served on hiring committees, whether faculty searches or
  industry research hiring.

To nominate someone, or yourself, email
[{{ w.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ w.contact_email }}).

## Sponsor

Sponsorship covers the room and catering, and gives sponsors time with
candidates during the workshop. For current tiers, email
[{{ w.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ w.contact_email }}).

## Code of conduct

Everyone at the workshop is expected to follow the
[SIGARCH CARES]({{ w.code_of_conduct_url }}) policies. Concerns can be raised
with any organizer in the room or with the CARES contacts directly.
